Implementation roadmap

I would have a hybrid life cycle to manage the work. Like Waterfall, it's important to have clear specifications and sign-offs for purchasing equipment and installing sensors. The dashboard and alert logic should be tested and improved in short cycles, however. The team would conduct a survey of rooms, check campus staff and feasibility before formal initiation. Planning, execution, monitoring and controlling and closing would be documented, as would be the initiation.
