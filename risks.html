06 / Risk & SWOT

Manage uncertainty early

RiskLikelihood / impactResponse

Likelihood and impact are preliminary judgments. A risk owner reviews each item monthly; high-severity issues are escalated to the sponsor.

Strengths

Continuous monitoring; measurable installation deliverables; reusable analytics.

Weaknesses

Maintenance burden; limited interpretation of CO₂; data quality dependence.

Opportunities

Facilities optimization; student research; later regional expansion.

Threats

Cyber incidents; vendor lock-in; budget changes; obsolete hardware.

Closing process

By December 31, 2029, the team will verify acceptance criteria, complete final inspections, reconcile contracts, transfer operating manuals and credentials, document lessons learned, and obtain formal sponsor sign-off. UFV operations would then assume ownership of ongoing monitoring and maintenance.
